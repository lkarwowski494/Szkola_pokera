# PokerGym — Privacy Policy

Effective [DO USTALENIA: publication date] (app version [DO USTALENIA: 1.0.0]).

## In short

The PokerGym app itself does not collect any data. Your learning progress stays on your iPhone. The app has no account, server, ads, analytics or tracking and sends no data to the developer. Purchases are handled by Apple (the App Store). Exception: an email you send us yourself (section “When you write to us”).

The app teaches poker decision-making. It has no gambling for money or chips.

## What is stored and where

The app’s database on your device (SQLite) stores only:

- learning progress: completed lessons, answers in exercises, test results, review cards and review history;
- settings: language, reminder (on/off and time), date of the last app rating prompt, date of the last export;
- the number of exercises started on a given day, with the date and your phone’s time zone (free version daily limit);
- the last known access status (purchased product and end date), so the app works offline;
- an error log: up to the 200 most recent entries with time, error code, screen name and app version. It contains no answers or personal data and is never sent anywhere.

No one but you, including the developer, has access to this data.

An iPhone backup (in iCloud or on a computer), if you turn it on in iOS, also includes the app’s data. It is subject to Apple’s terms.

On the device, the app reads your phone’s language, region and time zone to choose the language, show help numbers for your country and set the day boundary. It does not send them.

## Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple (the App Store). The app has no account or payment service of its own and never sees your payment details. It only reads from Apple which product is active and until when. You manage and cancel a subscription in your Apple account settings.

## Notifications

The study reminder is a local notification scheduled on your phone; it works only if you turn it on and allow notifications. The app does not use push notifications and does not send data to servers.

## Rating prompt

The app may occasionally show the system App Store rating prompt. Apple handles the prompt; the app does not receive your rating or review.

## External links

Some buttons open pages outside the app: Apple’s terms, the app’s App Store page, subscription management, and help services for people with gambling problems (the “Help” screen). These pages are governed by their owners’ terms.

## Exporting and importing progress

“Export progress” creates a file on your device with your learning progress and settings (without the error log or purchase status). The file contains activity dates and your phone’s time zone. Only you share it, for example via the iOS share sheet; once shared, the file is subject to the terms of the service you use. “Import progress” reads a file you choose.

## Deleting data

Delete your progress in the app: Settings → “Delete progress”. This removes lessons, answers, results, reviews, settings, the error log and the scheduled reminder. What stays: purchase status (purchases are handled by Apple) and the number of exercises started today (daily limit). Delete all app data by deleting the app from your phone. Delete export files wherever you saved them.

## Crash reports

If you choose in iOS settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may pass crash reports of the app to us under its own terms. The app has no crash-reporting mechanism of its own. The reports are used only to fix bugs.

## Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children. It does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## When you write to us

The app still sends nothing: you send the email, from your own mail app. The “Write to us” and “Report a problem with this task” buttons open your mail app; the problem report draft contains the task ID, app version and language. You see the whole draft before sending and can change it or not send it. If you write to support@apps4brew.com, we handle your message as follows:

- **Controller:** Łukasz Karwowski (seller of the app), natural person, not a business; contact: support@apps4brew.com.
- **Data:** your email address, the message text and attachments, and the draft details (task ID, app version, language).
- **Purpose:** replying to your message, clarification, fixing a bug in the app or in the course content.
- **Legal basis:** Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest: handling enquiries and improving the app).
- **Retention:** 12 months after the case is closed, then we delete the message. A bug description without personal data may remain in our fix notes.
- **Recipients and processors:** Google Workspace (Google) provides the mailbox for support@apps4brew.com as a processor, under Google’s data processing terms (Cloud Data Processing Addendum). We do not sell your data, share it for advertising or use it for advertising.
- **Transfers outside the EEA:** if Google processes messages outside the EEA, this takes place under the transfer mechanism adopted by Google or standard contractual clauses (section 4.1 of Google’s Cloud Data Processing Addendum) [DO USTALENIA: which mechanism applies to our account].
- **Your rights:** access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection to processing, and a complaint to a supervisory authority, in particular in the country where you live (in Poland: the President of the Personal Data Protection Office, PUODO). Write to support@apps4brew.com; we will reply without undue delay and within one month at the latest.
- **Do you have to provide data:** writing to us is voluntary. Without an email address we cannot reply.
- **Automated decisions:** we make no decisions based solely on automated processing, including profiling.
